About the game
  • Investigate each character and examine their unique features throughout the game.
  • Carefully check every suspect's alibi and analyze the details, as each character contains hidden clues.
  • Compare statements and observe inconsistencies to determine who is innocent or suspicious.
  • The Player is given three hearts, and each mistake will reduce one heart.
  • When all hearts are used, the player must wait for a 30-minute cooldown before the hearts are fully restored.
  • Successfully solving the first case will unlock the second case.
  • Completing the second case will unlock the third and final case.
  • Use logic, observation, and deduction skills to solve each case and progress through the game.
